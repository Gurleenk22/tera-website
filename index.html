Our Programs & Services

  • Education:
    To advance education by offering online and in-person workshops, classes, summer camps, seminars, conferences, symposiums, exhibitions, etc. on topics such as technology, art, financial literacy, entrepreneurship, leadership, communication, employment readiness, tailored for multicultural children, youth, women, adults, and seniors.
  • Arts & Culture:
    To advance public appreciation of the arts by organizing public art exhibitions, presentations, or performance art events, while creating a platform for multicultural youth and adult artists to showcase, present, or perform their artistic creations.
  • Senior Support:
    To support seniors by offering social and educative initiatives that address challenges such as loneliness, isolation, depression, fear, and abuse while enhancing their mental and emotional well-being, empowering them to lead healthier, more connected lives.
